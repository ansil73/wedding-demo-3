How do guests open the invitation?

You send one link on WhatsApp. It opens in the phone browser. Nothing to install.

Do you do Christian and Muslim weddings too?

Yes. We adapt the wording, events and colours, for example church and reception, or nikah and walima.

Guests can reply to you on WhatsApp from the invitation. Replies are not stored on the website.
